2. What WDIL Does

WDIL records when you last did something. From the dates you log, it works out how often you usually do each thing and tells you when one of them is probably due again. You can also set a fixed interval yourself, in which case the App uses that instead.

Everything the App shows about timing is an observation drawn from your own entries. It is an estimate, not a schedule, and it can be wrong. WDIL is not a medical device, a therapeutic tool, or a substitute for professional advice, and it makes no claims about health outcomes or habit change.
