WDIL

When did I last…?

WDIL records when you last did something. From the dates you log, it works out how often you usually do each thing and tells you when one of them is probably due again. You can also set a fixed interval yourself, in which case the App uses that instead.

Everything it shows about timing is an observation drawn from your own entries — an estimate, not a schedule.

Your data stays with you

  • No account, no username, no password.
  • Everything you log is stored on your device, not on a server.
  • Free for up to five items; a one-time purchase removes the limit.
  • No subscription, no auto-renewal, no ads.
